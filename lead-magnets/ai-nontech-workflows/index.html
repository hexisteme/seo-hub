🧭 Why 1-Click Workflows?

Most AI tutorials teach you to write 500-word prompts with complex constraints. In practice, that is the slowest way to get work done.

The most productive founders and professionals in 2026 don't chat with general AI models for hours. They use purpose-built, 1-click structured tools that turn raw inputs into finished assets instantly.

📋 The 10 "Fill-in-the-Blank" Everyday AI Prompts

When you *do* need to use ChatGPT or Claude, use these rigid templates instead of writing from scratch:

  1. Jargon-to-English Translator: "Explain [COMPLEX TOPIC] in 3 bullet points as if I am a customer deciding whether to buy."
  2. Email De-escalation Filter: "Rewrite this draft to be firm, polite, and under 50 words: [DRAFT]"
  3. Contract Clause Scanner: "List any clauses in this agreement that renew automatically or impose financial penalties: [TEXT]"
  4. Meeting Action-Item Extractor: "Extract only decisions made and tasks assigned with owners from this transcript: [TRANSCRIPT]"
  5. Customer Objection Counter: "Give me 3 calm, respectful responses to a client saying '[OBJECTION]'."
  6. Competitive Pricing Teardown: "Extract the pricing tiers, hidden limits, and transaction fees from these three competitor descriptions: [TEXT]"
  7. Social Carousel Outliner: "Turn this short article into a 5-slide visual carousel with big bold hooks: [ARTICLE]"
  8. Late Invoice Reminder: "Draft a polite but firm 2-sentence follow-up on invoice #[NUM] which is 14 days overdue: [DETAILS]"
  9. Executive Summary Generator: "Summarize this 10-page document into a 1-paragraph executive brief for busy stakeholders: [DOCUMENT]"
  10. Software Waste Audit: "Identify any redundant tools or overlapping features in this list of team subscriptions: [LIST]"

10 Practical Non-Tech AI Prompts & Workflow Blueprints

A companion prompt pack for The 1-Click AI Starter Pack and The Non-Tech AI Workflow Vault ($19). Copy and paste directly into Claude, ChatGPT, or Notion AI. No prompt engineering experience required.

1. The Executive Pitch Deck Blueprint (Gamma & Claude)

Role: Senior Presentation Designer & Executive Storyteller.
Task: Convert the raw meeting notes and bullet points below into a structured 10-slide presentation outline ready for 1-click generation in Gamma.

Input Notes:
[PASTE RAW NOTES OR BULLETS HERE]

Requirements:
1. Divide into exactly 10 distinct slide cards:
   - Slide 1: High-Impact Hook & Problem Statement
   - Slide 2: The Hidden Market Friction
   - Slide 3: Our Unfair Advantage / Solution
   - Slide 4: How It Works (3 Steps)
   - Slide 5: Unit Economics & Pricing Model
   - Slide 6: Traction & Milestones
   - Slide 7: Competitive Landscape (Why Others Fail)
   - Slide 8: Go-To-Market & Distribution Plan
   - Slide 9: The Core Team
   - Slide 10: Clear Next Step / Call to Action
2. For each slide, output:
   - Slide Title (under 6 words)
   - 3 bullet points (maximum 15 words per bullet)
   - 1 Visual Layout suggestion (e.g., 3-column cards, comparison table, stat callout).

2. The 1-Click Local Business Website Copy (Durable & ChatGPT)

Role: Conversion Copywriter for Small Businesses.
Task: Generate the complete text copy for a 1-page business website to input into an AI site builder.

Business Name: [BUSINESS NAME]
Industry / Niche: [e.g. Residential Plumbing, Dental Clinic, Boutique Fitness Studio]
Location / Service Area: [CITY, STATE]
Target Customer: [e.g. Homeowners aged 30-60]

Output Format:
1. Hero Header (under 8 words) + Sub-headline (under 20 words) + Primary CTA button text.
2. 3 Core Value Propositions with title and 2-sentence benefit description.
3. 3 Featured Service Packages with simple pricing descriptions.
4. "Why Choose Us" trust box with 4 bullet points (guarantees, licensing, local experience).
5. FAQ section with 3 common client questions and calm, reassuring answers.

3. The Viral Shorts Repurposing Matrix (OpusClip & Descript)

Role: Short-Form Video Producer.
Task: Analyze this long-form podcast or video transcript and extract 5 distinct 45-second vertical short concepts.

Transcript:
[PASTE TRANSCRIPT EXCERPT]

1. Hook Line (0-3s): A controversial, counter-intuitive, or high-curiosity statement.

4. The Customer Service Auto-Responder (Make.com & Claude)

Role: Empathetic Customer Support Specialist.
Task: Triage and draft a respectful, solution-oriented response to an inbound customer message.

Inbound Message:
[PASTE CUSTOMER EMAIL / TICKET]

Instructions:
1. Sentiment Classification: [Positive / Neutral / Frustrated / Urgent Bug / Billing Dispute]
2. Priority Score: [Low / Medium / High / Critical]
3. 1-Sentence Internal Summary for the team.
4. Ready-to-Send Customer Reply:
   - Acknowledge their issue specifically without making vague corporate excuses.
   - Provide a clear 1-2 step resolution or confirmation of next actions.
   - Tone: Warm, professional, concise (under 120 words).

6. The Competitive Pricing Auditor (Perplexity Pro)

8. The Late Invoice Dispute Resolution Script

Role: Accounts Receivable Specialist.
Task: Draft a firm, courteous collection email for an overdue invoice.

Invoice Details:
- Client Name: [CLIENT]
- Invoice Number: [#1234]
- Amount Due: [$AMOUNT]
- Days Overdue: [e.g. 14 days / 30 days]

Draft 2 versions:
Version A (Friendly Reminder - 1-14 days overdue): Casual, assuming honest oversight.
Version B (Formal Demand - 30+ days overdue): Direct, outlining service suspension if unpaid within 5 business days.

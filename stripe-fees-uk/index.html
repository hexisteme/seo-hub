Stripe fees in the UK: what you pay, by card type and sale size

Fact-checked and signed by Seonrye Kim, who checks each figure against the provider's own published pricing page. · Updated 2026-09-04

  • Stripe's Standard plan charges 1.5% on a standard UK-issued card for an online payment, plus a per-transaction fee.
  • Stripe adds a 20p per-transaction fee on top of its percentage rate for a standard UK-issued card.
  • Stripe charges 2.8% on a premium UK-issued card for an online payment, instead of the 1.5% standard-card rate.
  • Stripe adds the same 20p per-transaction fee to a premium UK card as to a standard one.
  • Stripe charges 2.5% on a card issued in the European Economic Area for an online payment.

Stripe's UK fee for an online payment starts at 1.5% + 20p on a UK-issued card, and the merchant pays this on every sale. The rate is higher for a card issued in the EEA or elsewhere, and higher again when a sale needs currency conversion.

Taking the payment in person costs less

Stripe Terminal charges 1.4% on an EEA-issued card for an in-person payment, below the 2.5% it charges the same card online. A non-EEA card costs 2.9% in person, against 3.15% online. The per-transaction fee is lower too: 10p in person, against 20p online.

A currency conversion adds a surcharge

Stripe adds a 2% surcharge on top of whichever underlying rate applies: 2.5% for an EEA card, 3.15% for an international card.

A disputed payment costs £20.00 up front

Stripe charges £20.00 for every payment dispute it receives, regardless of the outcome. Responding to a dispute manually adds another £20.00, which Stripe refunds only if the merchant wins.

Both fees sit outside the percentage rate, so a business with a high dispute rate should budget for them separately.

What sets your Stripe UK rate

  1. Channel. Taking the payment in person through Stripe Terminal costs less than the same card online.
  2. Plan. The Standard plan has no monthly fee. A business with high volume or an unusual model can negotiate a custom package instead of the published rate.
  3. Average ticket. The percentage rate matters more as your average sale grows, and the flat 20p per-transaction fee matters more as it shrinks.
  4. Additional charges. A disputed payment has its own flat fee on top of the percentage rate, and general ways to cut card-processing costs apply in the UK too — see credit card processing fees.

FAQ

How much more does a premium UK card cost than a standard one on Stripe?

A premium UK card costs £3.00 on a £100 sale, at Stripe's 2.8% premium rate. Stripe decides which cards count as premium — a merchant can't opt out of the higher rate at checkout.

Is Bacs Direct Debit cheaper than a card payment on Stripe UK?

Bacs Direct Debit charges 1% of the payment. There's a 20p minimum on small amounts, and a £4.00 cap on large ones. The minimum stops adding to the fee once a sale reaches £20.00. The cap takes over, holding the fee flat, once a sale passes £400.00.

Does Stripe charge a monthly fee in the UK?

No. The Standard plan has no setup fee, no monthly fee and no hidden fees — a merchant only pays the percentage and per-transaction fee on each sale.

Is it cheaper to take a card payment in person than online with Stripe?

Yes. A £100 sale with an EEA-issued card costs £1.50 in person, on Stripe's 1.4% Terminal rate — below the 2.5% online rate for the same card. A non-EEA card costs £3.00 in person, on a 2.9% rate.

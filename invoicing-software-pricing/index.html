Pricing for invoicing software is split across two places. One is the monthly subscription fee. The other is the per-invoice fee triggered when a customer pays it. Vendors keep the two on separate pages.

Until you put them together, you cannot tell which vendor is cheaper. A business on a free plan that takes mostly card payments can end up paying more than one on a paid plan that takes bank transfers. The table below pulls both numbers from each vendor's official pages and sets them side by side.

One thing to flag up front: QuickBooks blocks automated retrieval of its pages, so the QuickBooks figures below are the only ones we could not check byte-for-byte against the live source. Those entries are marked unverified in the footnotes — not because we believe the values are wrong, but because we have no way to re-confirm them.

The cheapest plan and the cheapest bill are different questions

Read the table by row and one vendor looks cheap; read it by column and a different one does. That is not a quirk of these three products — it is what happens whenever a subscription fee and a per-transaction fee are quoted separately. A free plan charges nothing until an invoice is paid, and then charges on every one. A paid plan charges every month whether you invoice or not, and may or may not lower the per-transaction rate in exchange.

The crossover depends on two things the vendors cannot know: how many invoices you send, and how your customers pay them. Card payments and bank transfers are priced very differently here, and the gap between them is wide enough that steering customers toward bank transfer changes the answer more than switching vendors does.

The rates in the table are the rates before anything special happens

Each of the card rates above is a baseline that applies to an ordinary domestic card typed by nobody — the customer clicks a link and pays. Depart from that and the rate moves: a card keyed in by hand costs more, a premium or commercial card costs more, a payout pulled forward costs more, and an installment option costs a great deal more.

Only some of those surcharges are published on the pricing pages we cite. FreshBooks documents several of its own on a help-center article rather than the pricing page, and that article is rendered client-side in a way we could not capture as a literal quote — so those figures are deliberately absent from the table rather than cited from memory. They are listed among the open questions below, which is the honest place for a number we believe exists but cannot show you.

Two more prices worth flagging sit outside the table entirely. FreshBooks' custom-priced Select plan advertises a lower card rate than the standard 2.9% + $0.30, but the actual percentage is Not published anywhere on freshbooks.com. QuickBooks Online's free ACH bill-pay is not unlimited either — once a business uses up its plan's monthly allotment, each additional standard ACH transaction costs $0.50.

Two of these prices are promotional, and one page will not let us check it

The subscription prices we quote are regular prices, not the discounted first-term offers that these pages lead with. That distinction matters when comparing: a first-year promotional rate and a renewal rate can differ by an order of magnitude, and the number you will actually pay in year two is the regular one.

Separately, QuickBooks blocks automated retrieval of its pricing and payment-rate pages. Our figures came through a rendered-text extraction and were cross-checked against a web archive; that cross-check caught two quotes that were not literal and both were corrected. Every QuickBooks item is nonetheless labelled unverified in the footnotes, because no path we have re-reads the live page. If a QuickBooks price matters to your decision, click the source and read it yourself — that is the one verification we cannot perform for you.

FAQ

How much does FreshBooks cost per month?

At regular (non-promotional) pricing, FreshBooks' Lite plan is $23.00/mo, Plus is $43.00/mo, and Premium is $70.00/mo. A custom-priced Select plan is also available for larger teams by talking to a consultant. All three published tiers currently run limited-time promotional pricing well below these regular rates.

What are QuickBooks Online's monthly plan prices?

At regular, non-promotional pricing, QuickBooks Online's Simple Start plan is $38 per month, Essentials is $85 per month, and Plus is $140 per month; a higher-tier Advanced plan also exists with pricing shown on the same page. A limited-time introductory discount was running on all tiers at the time of research, so the price shown to a new signup will be lower for an initial period before reverting to these rates.

Is Wave accounting software really free?

Wave's Starter plan itself is $0/month with no subscription fee, but a paid Pro plan at $19 USD/month unlocks unlimited user seats, automated bank sync, and discounted card-processing rates on an early batch of transactions each month. Card and bank payment processing fees apply on both plans regardless of the subscription tier.

Which invoicing software has the lowest credit card processing fee: FreshBooks, QuickBooks, or Wave?

FreshBooks charges 2.9% + $0.30 per card transaction, Wave's free Starter plan charges 2.9% + $0.60 (or 2.9% + $0 for an early batch of transactions each month on Wave's paid Pro plan), and QuickBooks Payments charges a flat 2.99% with no added cents. On a small transaction, FreshBooks' lower flat fee ($0.30 vs $0.60) can beat both; on a larger transaction, QuickBooks' cents-free 2.99% can come out ahead of FreshBooks' 2.9%+$0.30 depending on the amount.

Do FreshBooks, QuickBooks, and Wave charge a fee for ACH/bank transfer payments?

Yes, all three charge 1% per transaction for ACH bank transfer payments: FreshBooks (1%, US customers only), QuickBooks Payments (1%), and Wave (1% with a $1.00 minimum fee). None of the three offer fee-free bank transfer payments on invoices.

Is it cheaper to use a free plan or to pay for one?

It depends less on the plan than on how your customers pay. A free plan costs nothing monthly but charges the full card rate on every paid invoice, so the bill scales with volume. A paid plan charges every month whether you invoice or not, and buys back some of the per-transaction cost. The larger lever is the payment method: on Wave a card payment costs 2.9% + $0.60 per credit card transaction while a bank transfer costs 1% per transaction ($1.00 minimum fee), and that gap is wide enough that steering customers toward bank transfer usually changes the total more than switching plans does.

What does FreshBooks Advanced Payments cost and what does it do?

FreshBooks charges $20/month for the Advanced Payments (Virtual Terminal) add-on on its Lite, Plus, and Premium plans; the custom-priced Select plan marks it as Included at no extra charge.
